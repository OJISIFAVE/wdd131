Site Name

Pet Adoption and Animal Care Center

This name represents a place where people can adopt pets and learn how to properly care for animals.

Site Purpose

The website provides information about pet adoption, available pets, the adoption process, and responsible pet ownership. It also allows users to contact the center for adoption or volunteering opportunities.
